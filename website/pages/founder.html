About Anshuman

Anshuman Tripathy founded Beacon of Unity out of a simple frustration shared by most people who've ever lived in a new city: the neighbourhood WhatsApp group that's either dead silent or completely unusable — buried notifications, no way to tell who's actually verified, and genuinely useful posts (a lost passport, a volunteer drive, someone offering to help) lost in noise within hours.

Rather than trying to fix that inside the apps people already have, he set out to build something UAE communities didn't have yet: a single platform where every member is verified against a real Emirates ID before they can post or respond to anything — no anonymous accounts, no bots, no guesswork about who you're actually talking to. Everything else — help requests sorted by real distance, a proper lost & found flow, curated volunteer events, and a filtered feed of trusted local news — was built around that one non-negotiable: real identity first, everything else second.

Beacon of Unity is early — the platform you're looking at is still being actively built and refined — but the goal hasn't moved: give UAE residents a place where "community" means something more solid than a group chat.
